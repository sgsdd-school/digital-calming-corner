Understanding the zones

A quick guide to the four color zones used in the student corner.

The zones help children notice their energy and alertness. They are not labels for good or bad behavior. A child can move between zones, and the aim is to notice what they need.

Blue Zone

Low energy · Low alertness

A child may feel tired, slow, sleepy, or low in energy.

Green Zone

Just right · Calm and focused

A child may feel calm, settled, ready to learn, and able to focus.

Yellow Zone

High energy · High alertness

A child may feel excited, worried, frustrated, restless, or fidgety.

Red Zone

Very high energy · Overwhelmed

A child may feel very angry, frightened, out of control, or overwhelmed.

Gray · Not Sure

Unsure how I feel · Need help noticing

This is not a fifth emotional zone. It is a support option for a child who cannot yet identify their feeling or energy level. The student app guides the child through a few simple questions, then suggests a color zone that may be the closest match. The child can choose a different zone if the suggestion does not feel right.

There is no “bad” zone. The colors are simply a way to talk about feelings and what might help. Gray is there for moments when a child is not sure yet.
